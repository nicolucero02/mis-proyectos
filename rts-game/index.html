Mini RTS

Controls

  • Left Click — Select unit or building
  • Drag — Box-select multiple units
  • Shift + Click — Add/remove from selection
  • Right Click — Move (ground) or Attack (enemy)
  • Edge Pan — Move mouse to screen edges to pan camera
  • Escape — Cancel placement / Deselect all

Economy

  • Gold — Workers automatically find and mine gold clusters, then return to Townhall
  • Food — Passively generated, caps your unit count

Goal

Gather gold, build a Barracks, train Soldiers, and destroy the enemy Townhall before they destroy yours.
